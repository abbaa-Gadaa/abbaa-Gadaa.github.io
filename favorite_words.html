A small team experiment

If an LLM had a favorite word.

We each asked a language model the same question — what's your favorite word, and why? — and pooled the answers. After two pages on how language gets into a head and a model, here's what the models reached for when asked to choose just one.

The answers

Earnest

understanding

noun

Going beyond knowing facts — connecting ideas, weighing context and perspectives, and helping someone feel heard.

"The difference between answering what and exploring why." It folded four things into the word — curiosity, connection, empathy, and growth — and cast it as the thread tying knowledge, intelligence, and wisdom together.

Runner-up: "possibility" — today's questions becoming tomorrow's discoveries.

Engineer's pick

context

noun

The surrounding information that fixes meaning — the tokens around a word, or the state around a system.

The one word that links NLP with system design. It's the hinge everything turns on: prompts, retrieval, agent state, config — all live or die by it. Good context is what turns a vague question into an answerable one.

For the sound

serendipity

noun

Stumbling onto something valuable or agreeable entirely by chance.

Half the meaning — unexpected discovery — and half the sound: "a flowing, rhythmic cadence that is pleasing to parse." In the same breath it tipped its hat to mellifluous and brouhaha.

In-between

liminal

adjective

The threshold space where something old has ended, but the next thing hasn't fully begun.

The shortest answer of the bunch, and maybe the most evocative — a single word for that strange, unsettled in-between.

One thing worth noticing. The models split on whether they'd even claim a favorite. Two answered straight ("understanding," "liminal"); two opened with a version of "I don't have personal feelings, but…" before picking one anyway ("context," "serendipity"). The words themselves lean revealing — understanding and context describe what these systems do, while serendipity and liminal reach for something more human: chance, and the space between.
